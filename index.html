    Price alerts The file ↗

    Checked at every monitor slot. Each level sends one phone alert when the price reaches it, then re-arms once the price moves 3% back, so a price hovering at the line does not keep alerting. Levels are in the quote currency: pence for London shares, as Trading 212 shows them. Missing or stale prices never alert.

    Track record
    More detail: by market, score and time held

    Every alert is logged with the price at the time, then checked against what happened next and against its home index over the same period (FTSE 100 for London shares, Euro Stoxx 50 for European ones, else the S&P 500). For greed and trim alerts, a fall afterwards counts as the right call. The hit rate switches to one-month results once there are enough of them.

    Swing paper trading

    Every clean swing setup from the nightly index scan is logged as a paper trade under the swing rules: buy at the next open, stop at 2× the average daily range, take profit at twice the risk (2R), or sell after 20 sessions. Average R is the result per trade in units of risk: above zero means the rules make money before costs. No real money is involved.

    Rebound watch

    Big names at least 8% below their 10-day high. "Rebound" marks those that also rose last session: a prompt to research, not a buy signal. One up day after a fall is often a dead-cat bounce.
